# Interface

In order to reduce friction, the vault's structure must be analogous to that observed through the subjective experience of looking up and navigating knowledge in the mind

the vault is divided into multiple directories, each representing, mirroring, projecting, exemplifying something

the separation is intuitive and the selection of the things is spontaneous sense of depth (am I referring to ease of access, a sense of primacy or significance, or a compromise between the former and the latter, idk)

the structure of each directory is shaped by the interest it represents and is eventually described by a `structure` file like this one

i think this approach is flexible; only bound by a hierarchical structure that's already optimised such that structural depth corresponds to a subjective sense of navigation depth, making the navigation of the structure analogous to that of the mind, thereby reducing friction

# Note types

This is not a rule, this is an observation, and an attempt to attach descriptive labels to what's observed, the list may be updated if new

 + a seed note: questions, observations, trains of thought, immediate otherwise short-lived intuitions and ideas, links to resources, etc.. whatever spark interest/provoke thought
 + a workbench note: a mess of resources to chew on, brain dumps, and partially digested ideas: an intellectual diarrhea..., it's the next stage of development of "seed" notes
 + an article note: the result of organising and refining a thoroughly developed "workbench" note, or the over-development of an "moc" note
 + an moc/gallery note: a set of links sharing a theme, this may develop into an article making sense of the relations between the otherwise fragmented articles, drawing a big picture, explaining a higher level concept
